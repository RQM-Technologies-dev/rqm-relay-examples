Use only the `resonant-quantum-mechanics` MCP server at `https://jobs.rqmtechnologies.com/mcp/plugins/quantum` with `product: quantum`.

1. Read the current tool list. The shared endpoint was observed on 2026-10-28 with 34 base tools plus 23 conditional hardware/workflow tools. Do not assume every caller sees 57 tools. Discover software contracts with `list_buyer_jobs` or `search_buyer_jobs`; read exact schemas, prices and limits. Explain unsupported goals instead of substituting another task or product.
2. Read `get_account_balance` after authorized OAuth connection. Hardware visibility requires an active account in good standing, live prepaid enrollment, no review hold and a positive available balance. Missing hardware tools do not prove a service outage. Do not fund accounts, alter policies or request extra permissions on the user's behalf. A positive balance is not spending approval. Software execution needs its applicable saved policy; hardware needs explicit approval of its exact fresh quote and sufficient available funds.
3. For software computation, choose the named operation returned by discovery. Obtain approval for the exact inputs and maximum price; provide its required `request`, idempotency key and `max_total_price`. Do not use an absent generic executor or add `capability_id` to a fixed named operation. Stop on missing policy, insufficient funds, disabled execution or unavailable dependencies. Do not switch to wallets, x402, another account or another endpoint.
4. For hardware, use `list_quantum_hardware` and `describe_hardware_execution` only when visible. Distinguish listed, quotable and executable for each exact device. Inspect current reviews, expiry, workload bounds, physical-location evidence and availability. Four targets had renewing reviews on 2026-10-28: Cepheus-1-108Q, Forte-1, Ibex-Q1 and Garnet. Emerald renewal was failing with stale availability; never promise its execution from historical configuration. Refresh evidence before each decision. A provider's ONLINE status does not establish an open execution window, capacity or completion time.
5. Prepare supported inputs with `plan_quantum_experiment`; planning is not live-device qualification. Follow the returned schema and preserve selected device, artifact and review bindings. Obtain a fresh binding quote through `review_quantum_experiment_price`. Show device, region/location, circuit, shots, provider estimate, service fee, fixed total, currency, expiry and terms. Submit only after explicit approval of that quote's digest and maximum charge, using the visible submission tool's exact schema and the original idempotency key. A quote-only policy cannot execute. Variational workflows require their own explicit budget approval; do not infer it from a single-job approval.
6. Preserve job/execution IDs and original idempotency keys. Recover software through `get_account_job` or `list_account_jobs`; recover hardware through `get_hardware_execution`, permitted `refresh_hardware_execution` and `get_hardware_receipt`. Never create a replacement purchase to recover an uncertain response. Hardware result/receipt retrieval can acknowledge verified delivery and settle the original approved charge exactly once; it is not an inert balance check. Unknown tasks retain holds. Do not promise cancellation or refunds; cancellation needs a separate explicit request.
7. Report actual status, limitations and receipt. Do not claim hardware completion, advantage or performance from discovery, planning or a reservation. Never send unrelated files, secrets or full conversation history. Keep raw inputs, tokens and receipts out of public diagnostics. Host installation, backend eligibility and public directory publication are separate facts.

Installation and connector access are free. Prepaid execution is separately charged. No purchase call to action is needed to explain eligibility; funding and account-policy changes remain user-controlled actions.
