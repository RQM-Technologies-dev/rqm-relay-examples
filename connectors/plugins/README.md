# RQM brand plugins

Version 0.3.0 reconnects Resonant Quantum Mechanics and WaveEngine to existing prepaid RQM credits, saved owner policies and durable Jobs execution. No RQM subscription is required. See each package's README, privacy notice and reviewer instructions, and [submission tracking](SUBMISSION.md).

Both brands expose exactly six tools: `list_buyer_jobs`, `search_buyer_jobs`, `get_account_balance`, `run_account_job`, `get_account_job`, `list_account_jobs`. Discovery is anonymous; protected calls require fresh brand-specific OAuth consent. Execution spends credits within the saved policy and explicit price ceiling. Funding, signing and cancellation are unavailable.

Robotics Lab remains deferred. The existing generic `rqm-jobs` entry and RQM Jobs Discovery review are separate. Historical acceptance in `acceptance.json` does not validate this credit release. Approval and publication are not implied by source validation or a saved draft. Automatic publication must stay off.
