# RQM brand plugins

The current target is **Resonant Quantum Mechanics**, **WaveEngine** and **Robotics Lab** across ChatGPT, Cursor and Claude. See the [nine-cell readiness inventory](READINESS.md) for separate evidence stages and the executable unpaid audit. Earlier two-brand release scope and historical acceptance do not establish this expanded target.

Version 0.3.0 reconnects Resonant Quantum Mechanics and WaveEngine to existing prepaid RQM credits, saved owner policies and durable Jobs execution. No RQM subscription is required. See each package's README, privacy notice and reviewer instructions, and [submission tracking](SUBMISSION.md).

Both brands expose exactly six tools: `list_buyer_jobs`, `search_buyer_jobs`, `get_account_balance`, `run_account_job`, `get_account_job`, `list_account_jobs`. Discovery is anonymous; protected calls require valid brand-specific OAuth consent; new consent remains an owner action. Execution spends credits within the saved policy and explicit price ceiling. Funding, signing and cancellation are unavailable.

Robotics Lab currently retains its earlier shared discovery package; the separate prepaid route and current host acceptance are missing. The existing generic `rqm-jobs` entry and RQM Jobs Discovery review are separate. Historical acceptance in `acceptance.json` does not validate this credit release. Approval and publication are not implied by source validation or a saved draft. Automatic publication must stay off.
